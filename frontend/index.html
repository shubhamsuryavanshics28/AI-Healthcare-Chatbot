    💬

    Evidence-Grounded AI Chat

    Conversational agent utilizing AsyncOpenAI with clinical guardrails: strictly non-prescriptive, non-diagnostic, and grounded in official medical literature.

    📚

    Medical RAG Pipeline

    ChromaDB vector database coupled with PyMuPDF document extraction. Retrieves verified guidance from WHO, CDC, MedlinePlus, and India MoHFW.

    🚨

    Deterministic Emergency Safety

    Independent rule engine that screens for critical warning signs (cardiac, respiratory, FAST stroke) and instantly escalates to Emergency 112 before LLM execution.

    ⚖️

    4-Tier Triage Protocol

    Categorizes patient concerns into Emergency, Urgent Care, Routine Consultation, or Supportive Self-Care with transparent clinical rationales.

    💊

    Verified Medicine Directory

    Searchable pharmaceutical reference providing generic names, indications, serious warnings, and side effects from FDA and MedlinePlus.

    🔒

    Patient Data Isolation

    Argon2 password hashing, JWT authorization, and strict per-user database scoping for patient profile, allergies, and medication history.

    🔬 How the Triage and RAG Pipeline Works

    1

    Patient Consultation & Symptom Input

    The patient shares symptoms through the interactive chat or structured assessment wizard, specifying duration, severity, and location.

    2

    Deterministic Emergency Screen

    The safety engine intercepts text using clinical heuristics. Red flags trigger immediate emergency 112 guidance, bypassing LLM delays.

    3

    Semantic RAG Document Retrieval

    ChromaDB executes hybrid semantic search on official medical PDFs extracted via PyMuPDF, filtering results with strict relevance thresholds.

    4

    Grounded Synthesis & Triage Categorization

    The assistant synthesizes answers strictly grounded on cited sources, providing structured urgency level and next-step clinical recommendations.
